RelateIQ: Customer 360 + Next Best Action Engine (PS2) for a digital lender. RMs guess which of 2,000 borrowers need attention daily; disputes get dunned, churners slip away. Solution: 3 modular CoCo-compatible skills. 360 Profiler fuses accounts, transactions and repayments into explainable Attention + Opportunity scores. Signal Reader extracts intent (churn, dispute, upsell) from call transcripts and emails. Next-Best-Action Orchestrator assigns per-customer actions (dispute-hold, churn callback, collections nudge, retention offer, upsell outreach), drafts messages, and logs every decision to an append-only audit table. Streamlit app (Priority Feed / Customer 360 / Action Queue) runs offline in demo mode on synthetic data (streamlit + pandas only), or live on Snowflake with a Cortex Analyst model. Expected impact: daily queue in seconds, disputed borrowers protected, consistent outreach, clearer upsell visibility. The engine recommends; credit decisions stay with policy. Solo build, working prototype.
