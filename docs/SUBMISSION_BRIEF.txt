RelateIQ — Customer 360 + Next Best Action Engine (PS2) for a digital lender. Problem: RMs guess which of 2,000 borrowers need attention daily; disputes get dunned, churners slip away. Solution: 3 modular CoCo skills — 360 Profiler fuses accounts/transactions/repayments into Attention + Opportunity scores (explainable weighted model); Signal Reader extracts intent (churn, dispute, upsell) from call transcripts + support emails; Next-Best-Action Orchestrator picks per-customer actions (dispute-hold, churn callback, collections nudge, retention offer, pre-approved upsell), drafts outreach, and writes every decision to an append-only audit table. Streamlit app (Priority Feed / Customer 360 / Action Queue) runs in demo mode on synthetic data with just two dependencies (streamlit, pandas), or live on Snowflake with a Cortex Analyst semantic model. Projected impact: prioritized daily queue in seconds, zero dispute-duns, DSO down, upsell conversion up. Solo build, fully working prototype.
