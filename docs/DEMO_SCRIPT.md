# RelateIQ - 90-Second Live Demo Script

**Setup before the clock starts:** browser open on the Streamlit app (Demo mode), Priority Feed tab, sidebar visible. Anchor date 2026-10-06. Keep the cursor still unless a cue says click.

---

## BEAT 1 - THE PAIN (0:00–0:15) · ~15 seconds

**On screen:** blank slide or a simple title card reading "2,000 borrowers. One relationship manager. Every morning."

**Presenter words (verbatim):**
> Arjun Mehta is a relationship manager at a digital lender. Every morning he faces two thousand borrowers and one question: who needs attention today, and exactly what should I do about each one. Today, he guesses. Disputes get dunned. Churners slip away. Upsell windows close before anyone notices.

**Clicks:** none. Let the title card land.

---

## BEAT 2 - THE RUN (0:15–0:45) · ~30 seconds

**On screen:** the app, Tab 1 "Priority Feed". Sidebar shows "RelateIQ", "Arjun Mehta · Relationship Manager", Demo mode.

**Presenter words (verbatim):**
> This is RelateIQ, a Customer 360 plus Next Best Action engine. Watch the Priority Feed. Forty customers, ranked in seconds by an Attention Score from zero to a hundred, red critical down to green healthy. Sneha Iyer is at the top, delinquent, five missed payments. Kavya Nair sits at the bottom, a perfect upsell candidate. Expand any row and the engine shows its work: every score component, every trace line, nothing hidden.

**Click cues:**
1. Click the Priority Feed tab (already open).
2. Scroll once through the ranked table so the red-to-green gradient is visible.
3. Click Sneha Iyer's row to expand the reasoning trace; point at two or three trace lines.

---

## BEAT 3 - THE BRANCH (0:45–1:10) · ~25 seconds

**On screen:** Tab 2 "Customer 360" with Amit Verma selected, then the Action Queue.

**Presenter words (verbatim):**
> Now the part that protects the business. Amit Verma was double-charged. The Signal Reader found the dispute in his support emails, and the orchestrator chose dispute-hold: no outreach, internal review only. If we had dunned him, we would have torched a good customer. Meanwhile Vikram Rao, dormant ninety days, gets a human callback, and Kavya gets a pre-approved top-up. Six decision branches, one per customer, all in the queue with drafts ready to approve.

**Click cues:**
1. Click the Customer 360 tab.
2. Select Amit Verma from the selectbox; pause on the "dispute-open" signal badge and the dispute-hold action.
3. Click the Action Queue tab; scroll the queued decisions and one draft message.

---

## BEAT 4 - THE IMPACT (1:10–1:30) · ~20 seconds

**On screen:** Action Queue, scrolled to the audit log at the bottom. Approve one draft before this beat if time allows.

**Presenter words (verbatim):**
> And every approved action lands here, in an append-only audit table, timestamped with the score and the reason. RelateIQ turns a morning of guesswork into a ranked queue in seconds, with zero dispute-duns, a full compliance trail, and projected wins on delinquency and upsell conversion. Demo mode runs on synthetic data today; flip one toggle and the same engine runs live on Snowflake.

**Click cues:**
1. Scroll down to the audit log table; point at the timestamp, customer, action, and reason columns.
2. Flip the sidebar Demo/Live toggle briefly, show the fallback warning, flip back. (End on the working demo.)

**Closing line (optional, if the timer allows):**
> Arjun no longer guesses. He just opens the feed.
